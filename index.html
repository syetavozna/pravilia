Pravilia Autopilot

A private publishing tool for the Pravilia animated shorts series.

Pravilia Autopilot is a personal tool used by the creator of the Pravilia channels. It uploads the channel's own finished videos — together with their titles, descriptions and tags — to the creator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos to the creator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail and the "AI-generated content" label.
  • Posts the creator's own first comment under the video.

The tool is not offered to the public and acts only on the accounts of its owner.
