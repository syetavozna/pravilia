Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Pravilia Autopilot ("the app") is a personal tool that publishes videos to its owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google or TikTok, the app receives OAuth access tokens. It uses them only to upload videos, set video details and thumbnails, post the owner's own comments, and read basic account information needed to publish.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, does not collect analytics, and does not store data about any other person.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to Google (YouTube Data API) and TikTok (Content Posting API) to perform the actions described above.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in TikTok settings under "Security → Manage app permissions".
